A reproducible machine-learning workflow for predicting when a customer is likely to leave a service based on behavioral data.

The project uses statistical feature selection, XGBoost, and Bayesian hyperparameter optimization. The supplied analytics resume records an 85% evaluation accuracy; the more conservative engineering resume describes the workflow without relying on that number, so this page treats it as a resume-reported result rather than an independently reproduced benchmark.
